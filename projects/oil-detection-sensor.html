PROJECT CASE STUDY / 04

Oil Detection Sensor

ESP32-based oil leakage monitoring prototype. A practical engineering project exploring technology-driven problem solving.

01. Project Overview

An embedded monitoring prototype designed to detect oil presence or leakage and generate real-time alerts for faster attention.

02. Problem Statement

Oil leakage can affect equipment operation and workplace safety. A monitoring approach that detects oil presence and quickly communicates an alert can help users respond sooner.

03. Working Principle

The ESP32-based prototype reads sensor information, evaluates the detected condition and triggers an alert when oil is identified. The résumé describes the system as an oil leakage monitoring prototype; exact sensor model and alert channel can be added from the circuit or project report.

04. Outcome & Impact

The résumé reports approximately 92% detection accuracy and alerts within 3 seconds during controlled testing.

The performance figures above are taken from the résumé. They should be interpreted in the context of the stated controlled or simulated testing.

05. Learning & Engineering Focus

  • Applying embedded-system concepts to a practical use case.
  • Integrating sensor inputs with a microcontroller-based control approach.
  • Considering reliability, response time and real-world usability.
  • Developing practical problem-solving and prototyping skills.
