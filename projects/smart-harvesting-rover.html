PROJECT CASE STUDY / 02

Smart Harvesting Rover

Sensor-guided agricultural rover prototype. A practical engineering project exploring technology-driven problem solving.

01. Project Overview

A sensor-guided rover concept designed to support automated crop harvesting and improve operational efficiency in agricultural environments.

02. Problem Statement

Manual harvesting can require considerable time and effort. This prototype explores how a mobile embedded platform and sensors can support selected harvesting operations.

03. Working Principle

The ESP32-based platform receives inputs from sensors and uses the embedded control system to guide the rover's operation. The design demonstrates the application of embedded systems to agricultural automation. Specific navigation, actuator and crop-handling details can be added from the project documentation.

04. Outcome & Impact

The résumé reports a functional prototype and a 30% reduction in simulated manual harvesting effort.

05. Learning & Engineering Focus

  • Applying embedded-system concepts to a practical use case.
  • Integrating sensor inputs with a microcontroller-based control approach.
  • Considering reliability, response time and real-world usability.
  • Developing practical problem-solving and prototyping skills.
